# Whisper replies by keyword and state (src/whisper_matrix.h). Format: keyword|state|text
# keyword: where, inv, group, lfg, level, help. state: grinding, questing, traveling, dungeon, fullgroup, or any. {level} is the bot's level.
# A state line is used when one fits and was not sent to this player in the last 10 minutes; else an "any" line; else the bot stays silent.
where|grinding|Out hunting around here, nothing special.
where|grinding|Just grinding nearby, killing whatever walks past.
where|questing|Working on some quests at the moment.
where|traveling|On the road to somewhere else right now.
where|dungeon|I am inside a dungeon with my group.
where|fullgroup|Out with my group, we are on a run.
where|any|Around, just busy at the moment.
inv|grinding|Sorry, I am in the middle of a grind. Maybe later.
inv|questing|I am busy with a quest right now, try me again in a bit.
inv|traveling|I am on my way somewhere, ask again when I arrive.
inv|dungeon|Sorry, I am in a dungeon, cannot take an invite now.
inv|fullgroup|My group is full, sorry.
inv|any|Not right now, sorry.
group|grinding|Maybe later, I am grinding at the moment.
group|questing|I am on a quest, but ask again later.
group|dungeon|In a dungeon already, sorry.
group|fullgroup|Our group is full, sorry.
group|any|Not at the moment, sorry.
lfg|dungeon|Already in a dungeon with a group.
lfg|fullgroup|Already grouped and full, thanks.
lfg|any|Maybe, but I am busy now. Try again later.
level|any|I am level {level}.
level|grinding|Level {level} and grinding.
help|any|I cannot do much, but ask in the open chat, someone may help.
help|dungeon|Busy in a dungeon right now, sorry.
