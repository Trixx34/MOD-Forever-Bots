# Rotation spec: spells by name, resolved to real ids and levels by tools/make_rotations.py (python3 -I tools/make_rotations.py --dump Server.log --spec
# data/rotation_spec.txt > data/rotations.txt). Rows are in priority order; buffs are upkeep spells cast out of combat when they have run out.
# Durations are the rank 1 lengths in seconds (a longer real duration only means a recast earlier than needed). Conditions: always, target_hp_below N,
# self_hp_below N, self_power_below N, self_power_above N, enemies N, target_casting, opener N, target_strong N, combo N.
# Left out on purpose: stances and forms, pets, totems that need placing, crowd control and anything that needs a target's target, the aura of a
# creature or a position behind a target. The party heals are done by the policy itself (heal block), not here.
# Rows marked "Forever" are abilities of this fork's client data (the Classic beta's Spell/SkillLineAbility/SpellEffect tables: class skill line, cost,
# cooldown, range and effects were read from them) that the vanilla kits lack. They are spells by name like the others: a name that the core's ability
# dump does not show for the class is dropped with a warning, so a bot never gets a guessed id. See doc/rotations.md for what was not added and why.
